What matters more is the tools and skills</strong></h2><p>Nous Research recently got called out by tech youtuber Theo for having many useless skills like Polymarket skill. I say having too many skills = a lot better than not having enough skills for Hermes.</p><p>Hermes auto-creates a skill every time he realizes that you&#8217;ll be running the same workflow again in the future.
