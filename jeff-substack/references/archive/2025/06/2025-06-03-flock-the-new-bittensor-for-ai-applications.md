Trainers improve models by submitting data &amp; gradients, leading to improvements in model performance / fewer hallucinations <em>(trainers are similar to miners in the Bittensor ecosystem)</em>.</p><p>Validators score the model based on the gradients that trainers submit.</p><ul><li><p>Trainers &amp; Validators need to <strong>stake $FLOCK to gmFLOCK</strong> before they can participate (choose from 0 to 365 days of lockup).</p></li><li><p>Trainers &amp; Validators with higher gmFLOCK get access to more tasks and a higher rewards multiplier (each has its own criteria for $FLOCK incentives, and gmFLOCK stake is one of the criteria).</p></li><li><p>Both Trainers &amp; Validators can get their gmFLOCK slashed if they perform maliciously (training submissions fail validation, inaccurate validations).</p></li><li><p>Delegators (stakers—normal humans like us) can stake our $FLOCK into gmFLOCK and delegate them to Trainers or Validators.
